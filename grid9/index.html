See the reasoning

A solid outline for the pattern, a dashed one and a struck-through digit for what can go. Step by step walks a chain one link at a time.

Practise one technique

Read how it works, then solve a puzzle that needs it. From Naked Singles to Jellyfish.

Everything in one place

Your own notes, your own pace

A grey row for notes, a blue row for numbers. Mark only the digits that have two or three places left, the way Snyder notation works, and let the grid show you the patterns.

Know what to look for

Tap Hint and the panel lists every technique that has a hit right now, with a count. It answers “what can I do here?” without giving the move away.

Help in doses

Where? Which cells? Why? Apply. Take only as much help as you need, and see the reasoning drawn on the grid.

One technique at a time

Pick a technique, read how it works, then solve a puzzle that actually needs it. Two dozen techniques, from Naked Singles to Jellyfish.

See what you really know

For every technique: how often you found it yourself, peeked at it, or took it from a hint. Stored on your device only.

Light, dark, and easy to read

It follows your phone, or you choose. Bigger numbers, highlighted rows, columns and boxes, all optional. Free for everyone.
